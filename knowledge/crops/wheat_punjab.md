# Wheat Cultivation Guide - Punjab Region

## Overview

Wheat (*Triticum aestivum*) is a major rabi crop in Punjab, typically sown in November and harvested in April-May.

## Optimal Planting Time

**Best Sowing Window**: November 1-20

- Early sowing (before November 1): Risk of pest infestation
- Late sowing (after November 20): Reduced yield potential
- Optimal temperature for germination: 20-25°C

## Soil Requirements

- **Soil Type**: Loamy to clay loam
- **pH Level**: 6.5-7.5
- **Drainage**: Well-drained fields essential

## Seed Rate and Variety

**Recommended Seed Rate**: 100 kg/hectare

**Popular Varieties in Punjab**:
- PBW 725: High yielding, rust resistant
- HD 3086: Suitable for late sowing
- DBW 187: Heat tolerant

## Irrigation Schedule

Wheat requires 4-6 irrigations depending on soil type and rainfall:

1. **Crown Root Initiation** (20-25 days after sowing)
2. **Tillering Stage** (40-45 days)
3. **Jointing Stage** (60-65 days)
4. **Flowering Stage** (80-85 days)
5. **Milk Stage** (100-105 days)
6. **Dough Stage** (115-120 days) - if needed

**Critical Stages**: Crown root initiation and flowering - never skip these irrigations.

## Fertilizer Application

**Basal Dose** (at sowing):
- Nitrogen: 60 kg/ha
- Phosphorus: 60 kg/ha
- Potassium: 30 kg/ha

**Top Dressing**:
- First: 60 kg N/ha at first irrigation
- Second: 60 kg N/ha at second irrigation

## Pest and Disease Management

### Common Pests
- **Termites**: Use chlorpyrifos 20 EC @ 2.5 L/ha
- **Aphids**: Spray dimethoate 30 EC @ 1 L/ha

### Common Diseases
- **Yellow Rust**: Use resistant varieties, spray propiconazole if needed
- **Brown Rust**: Spray mancozeb 75 WP @ 2 kg/ha

## Weed Control

- **Pre-emergence**: Pendimethalin @ 3.3 L/ha within 2 days of sowing
- **Post-emergence**: Sulfosulfuron @ 25 g/ha at 30-35 days

## Harvesting

**Maturity Indicators**:
- Grain moisture: 20-25%
- Grain hardness: Cannot be dented by thumbnail
- Straw color: Golden yellow

**Harvest Method**: Combine harvester recommended for timely harvest

## Expected Yield

- **Good Management**: 50-55 quintals/hectare
- **Average**: 40-45 quintals/hectare

## Weather Risks

### Frost Risk
- **Critical Period**: Flowering to grain filling
- **Mitigation**: Light irrigation before frost event

### Heat Stress
- **Risk Period**: March-April
- **Mitigation**: Ensure adequate irrigation, use heat-tolerant varieties

## Market Considerations

- **MSP (Minimum Support Price)**: Check current government rates
- **Best Selling Period**: April-May immediately after harvest
- **Storage**: Moisture content should be below 12% for safe storage
