# Rice Cultivation Guide - Punjab Region

## Overview

Rice (*Oryza sativa*) is the main kharif crop in Punjab, sown in May-June and harvested in October-November.

## Nursery Preparation

**Nursery Sowing Time**: Mid-May to early June

**Seed Rate**:
- Fine varieties: 20 kg/ha
- Coarse varieties: 25 kg/ha

**Nursery Area**: 400-500 m² for 1 hectare transplanting

## Transplanting

**Optimal Time**: 25-30 days old seedlings

**Spacing**:
- Fine varieties: 20 cm × 15 cm
- Coarse varieties: 20 cm × 20 cm

**Seedlings per Hill**: 2-3 seedlings

## Water Management

Rice is a water-intensive crop requiring continuous standing water:

- **After Transplanting**: Maintain 2-3 cm water for first week
- **Tillering to Flowering**: 5-7 cm standing water
- **Grain Filling**: 3-5 cm water
- **15 Days Before Harvest**: Drain fields

**Water Requirement**: 120-150 cm total

## Recommended Varieties

**Basmati**:
- Pusa Basmati 1121: Premium quality, 145 days
- Pusa Basmati 1509: Early maturing, 120 days

**Non-Basmati**:
- PR 126: High yielding, 145 days
- PR 129: Bacterial blight resistant

## Fertilizer Schedule

**Basal Application**:
- Nitrogen: 60 kg/ha
- Phosphorus: 30 kg/ha
- Potassium: 30 kg/ha
- Zinc Sulfate: 25 kg/ha

**Top Dressing**:
- First: 60 kg N/ha at tillering (20-25 days after transplanting)
- Second: 60 kg N/ha at panicle initiation (40-45 days)

## Pest Management

### Major Pests
- **Stem Borer**: Use cartap hydrochloride 50 SP @ 1 kg/ha
- **Leaf Folder**: Spray chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC @ 150 ml/ha
- **Brown Plant Hopper**: Use buprofezin 25 SC @ 1 L/ha

### Diseases
- **Bacterial Leaf Blight**: Use resistant varieties, spray copper oxychloride
- **Blast**: Spray tricyclazole 75 WP @ 500 g/ha

## Weed Control

**Pre-emergence**: Pretilachlor 50 EC @ 1 L/ha within 3 days of transplanting

**Post-emergence**: Bispyribac sodium 10 SC @ 200 ml/ha at 15-20 days

## Harvesting

**Maturity Signs**:
- 80% grains turn golden yellow
- Grain moisture: 20-22%
- Duration: 120-150 days depending on variety

**Harvest Method**: Combine harvester or manual cutting

## Expected Yield

- **Basmati**: 30-35 quintals/hectare
- **Non-Basmati**: 60-70 quintals/hectare

## Weather Considerations

### High Temperature Stress
- **Critical Period**: Flowering stage
- **Impact**: Spikelet sterility
- **Mitigation**: Ensure adequate water, avoid water stress

### Heavy Rainfall
- **Risk**: Lodging, disease outbreak
- **Mitigation**: Balanced fertilization, proper drainage

## Environmental Concerns

**Water Conservation**:
- Direct Seeded Rice (DSR) saves 15-20% water
- Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) reduces water use

**Stubble Management**:
- Avoid burning - use Happy Seeder or incorporate with rotavator
- Government penalties for stubble burning

## Market Information

- **Basmati Premium**: Export quality fetches higher prices
- **MSP**: Check current government rates for non-basmati
- **Best Selling Period**: November-December
- **Storage**: Maintain moisture below 14%
